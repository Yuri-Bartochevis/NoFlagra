# NO FLAGRA — logo briefing

*A design brief for generating the NO FLAGRA logo. Written to be usable as-is
as a prompt for an image/logo generator, or as a brief for a human designer.*

---

## What the product is

A camera + physical button system for Brazilian jiu-jitsu gyms. A camera films
the mat continuously, 24/7. When anyone slaps a big button at the edge of the
mat, the system cuts the **last 10 minutes** out of the always-running
recording and saves it as a clip. Nothing starts recording on press — the
footage already exists. **The button is a pair of scissors, not a camera.**
Clips land in the gym's own cloud library, watchable from any phone.

## The name

"No flagra" is Brazilian slang — *caught in the act, on camera, at the exact
moment it happened*. The wordmark is styled as two words: **NO FLAGRA**
(currently rendered "No" in off-white, "Flagra" in yellow).

## Who it's for

Jiu-jitsu academy owners and their students in Brazil — the product and site
default to PT-BR, with English as a toggle. Mat-side, sweaty, practical. Not a
prosumer camera gadget: a fixture bolted to the wall of a gym.

## Brand personality

Loud, athletic, industrial, high-contrast. Broadcast / instant-replay energy —
sports TV, scoreboard, "REC" indicator. Blue-collar and durable, not soft or
startup-cute. Should read from across a training room.

## Existing visual identity (must match)

| | |
|---|---|
| Background | near-black `#0A0A0A`, panels `#161616`, borders `#2A2A2A` |
| Primary / accent | strong yellow `#FFD400` (highlight `#FFE666`, shadow `#C79A00`) |
| Off-white text | `#F5F5F0` |
| Alert red | `#FF3B30` — used for the recording dot |
| Green | `#3ECF8E` |
| Display typeface | **Anton** — heavy, condensed, uppercase, tight tracking |
| Body typeface | Inter |

Current placeholder mark: a dark rounded square with a yellow lightning bolt
inside. This is a stand-in — free to replace.

## What the mark should evoke

Pick and combine — don't cram all of it in:

- A big press-button / arcade-style button
- Rewind or jump-back symbolism (⏪, a looping arrow, "−10 min")
- An instant-replay / broadcast cue
- A subtle nod to the mat or grappling, if it doesn't get literal or cluttered

Avoid: generic film-reel clapperboards, generic security-camera housings,
kimono/belt clip art, MMA cage imagery.

## Deliverable

A logo lockup — icon mark plus the "NO FLAGRA" wordmark in heavy condensed
uppercase — plus a standalone icon that survives at 30×30 px (it's the site
header mark and the favicon).

Flat vector, hard edges. No gradients, no 3D bevels, no photorealism. Must work
yellow-on-black primarily, and stay legible as a single-color mark (pure
yellow, or pure black on yellow).

## Tagline (optional in the lockup)

- PT: *"Nunca perca o momento."*
- EN: *"Never miss the moment."*
